Marjorie Pajaron

I'm an economist studying weather shocks, migration, household welfare, and health economics, with a focus on the Philippines. I earned my PhD in Economics from the University of Hawai'i at Mānoa, and have held research fellowships at Stanford (Asia Health Policy Program) and the University of Manchester. I've spoken at conferences worldwide, including the Population Association of America, International Input-Output Association Conference, and the Global Labor Organization. When I'm not researching, I'm probably planning my next trip — Hawai'i will always be my second home.
